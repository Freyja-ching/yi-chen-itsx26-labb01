## English Security Summary

Several employees received emails that appeared to come from internal IT support. The emails asked recipients to use a link to keep access to an internal system. One employee clicked the link, but it is not confirmed whether any credentials were entered or whether an account was compromised. The emails may be part of a phishing attempt, but this also needs to be verified.

If an account is compromised, confidentiality, integrity and availability could be affected depending on the user's permissions. **If several employees are affected, their different permissions could also lead to different possible consequences.** The organization should first verify the email, sender and link. Relevant authentication and security logs should also be reviewed to identify suspicious activity. Finally, unnecessary user permissions should be limited to reduce the possible impact of a compromised account.
